For the following exercises, use the method of Lagrange multipliers to find the maximum and minimum values of the function subject to the given constraints.

\begin{enumerate}[start=1]
\item $f(x,y)=x^{2}y$, $x^{2}+2y^{2}=6$
\item $f(x,y,z)=xyz,x^{2}+2y^{2}+3z^{2}=6$
\item $f(x,y)=xy$, $4x^{2}+8y^{2}=16$
\item $f(x,y)=4x^{3}+y^{2}$, $2x^{2}+y^{2}=1$
\item $f(x,y,z)=x^{2}+y^{2}+z^{2},x^{4}+y^{4}+z^{4}=1$
\item $f(x,y,z)=yz+xy,xy=1,y^{2}+z^{2}=1$
\item $f(x,y)=x^{2}+y^{2},{(x-1)}^{2}+4y^{2}=4$
\item $f(x,y)=4xy,\frac{x^{2}}{9}+\frac{y^{2}}{16}=1$
\item $f(x,y,z)=x+y+z,\frac{1}{x}+\frac{1}{y}+\frac{1}{z}=1$
\item $f(x,y,z)=x+3y-z,x^{2}+y^{2}+z^{2}=4$
\item $f(x,y,z)=x^{2}+y^{2}+z^{2},xyz=4$
\item Minimize $f(x,y)=x^{2}+y^{2}$ on the hyperbola $xy=1$.
\item Minimize $f(x,y)=xy$ on the ellipse $b^{2}x^{2}+a^{2}y^{2}=a^{2}b^{2}$.
\item Maximize $f(x,y,z)=2x+3y+5z$ on the sphere $x^{2}+y^{2}+z^{2}=19$.
\item Maximize $\begin{array}{l}f(x,y)=x^{2}-y^{2},x>0,y>0; \\ g(x,y)=y-x^{2}=0\end{array}$
\item The curve $x^{3}-y^{3}=1$ is asymptotic to the line $y=x$. Find the point(s) on the curve $x^{3}-y^{3}=1$ farthest from the line $y=x$.
\item Maximize $U(x,y)=8x^{4/5}y^{1/5}$, $4x+2y=12$
\item Minimize $f(x,y)=x^{2}+y^{2},x+2y-5=0$.
\item Maximize $f(x,y)=\sqrt{6-x^{2}-y^{2}},x+y-2=0$.
\item Minimize $f(x,y,z)=x^{2}+y^{2}+z^{2},x+y+z=1$.
\item Minimize $f(x,y)=x^{2}-y^{2}$ subject to the constraint $x-2y+6=0$.
\item Minimize $f(x,y,z)=x^{2}+y^{2}+z^{2}$ when $x+y+z=9$ and $x+2y+3z=20$.
\end{enumerate}

For the next group of exercises, use the method of Lagrange multipliers to solve the following applied problems.

\begin{enumerate}[resume]
\item A pentagon is formed by placing an isosceles triangle on a rectangle, as shown in the diagram. If the perimeter of the pentagon is $10$ in., find the lengths of the sides of the pentagon that will maximize the area of the pentagon. \\ \textit{[Figure: A rectangle with an isosceles triangle on top. The side of the isosceles triangle with the two equal angles of size $\theta$ overlaps the top length of the rectangle.]}
\item A rectangular box without a top (a topless box) is to be made from $12\,\text{ft}^{2}$ of cardboard. Find the maximum volume of such a box.
\item Find the minimum and maximum distances between the ellipse $x^{2}+xy+2y^{2}=1$ and the origin.
\item Find the point on the surface $x^{2}-2xy+y^{2}-x+y=0$ closest to the point $(1,2,-3)$.
\item Show that, of all the triangles inscribed in a circle of radius $R$ (see diagram), the equilateral triangle has the largest perimeter. \\ \textit{[Figure: A circle with an equilateral triangle drawn inside of it such that each vertex of the triangle touches the circle.]}
\item Find the minimum distance from point $(0,1)$ to the parabola $x^{2}=4y$.
\item Find the minimum distance from the parabola $y=x^{2}$ to point $(0,3)$.
\item Find the minimum distance from the plane $x+y+z=1$ to point $(2,1,1)$.
\item A large container in the shape of a rectangular solid must have a volume of $480\,\text{m}^{3}$. The bottom of the container costs $\$5/\text{m}^{2}$ to construct whereas the top and sides cost $\$3/\text{m}^{2}$ to construct. Use Lagrange multipliers to find the dimensions of the container of this size that has the minimum cost.
\item Find the point on the line $y=2x+3$ that is closest to point $(4,2)$.
\item Find the point on the plane $4x+3y+z=2$ that is closest to the point $(1,-1,1)$.
\item Find the maximum value of $f(x,y)=\sin x\sin y$, where $x$ and $y$ denote the acute angles of a right triangle. Draw the contours of the function using a CAS.
\item A rectangular solid is contained within a tetrahedron with vertices at $(1,0,0),(0,1,0),(0,0,1)$, and the origin. The base of the box has dimensions $x$, $y$, and the height of the box is $z$. If the sum of $x$, $y$, and $z$ is $1.0$, find the dimensions that maximizes the volume of the rectangular solid.
\item \techrequired By investing x units of labor and y units of capital, a watch manufacturer can produce $P(x,y)=50x^{0.4}y^{0.6}$ watches. Find the maximum number of watches that can be produced on a budget of $\$20{,}000$ if labor costs \$100/unit and capital costs \$200/unit. Use a CAS to sketch a contour plot of the function.
\end{enumerate}
