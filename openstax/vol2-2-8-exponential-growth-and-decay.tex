True or False? If true, prove it. If false, find the true answer.

\begin{enumerate}[start=1]
\item The doubling time for $y=e^{ct}$ is $(\ln (2))/(\ln (c))$.
\item If you invest $\$500$, an annual rate of interest of $3\%$ yields more money in the first year than a $2.5\%$ continuous rate of interest.
\item If you leave a $100^\circ \text{C}$ pot of tea at room temperature $(25^\circ \text{C})$ and an identical pot in the refrigerator $(5^\circ \text{C})$, with $k=0.02$, the tea in the refrigerator reaches a drinkable temperature $(70^\circ \text{C})$ more than $5$ minutes before the tea at room temperature.
\item If given a half-life of t years, the constant $k$ for $y=e^{kt}$ is calculated by $k=\ln (1/2)/t$.
\end{enumerate}

For the following exercises, use $y=y_{0}e^{kt}$.

\begin{enumerate}[resume]
\item If a culture of bacteria doubles in $3$ hours, how many hours does it take to multiply by $10$?
\item If bacteria increase by a factor of $10$ in $10$ hours, how many hours does it take to increase by $100$?
\item How old is a skull that contains one-fifth as much radiocarbon as a modern skull? Note that the half-life of radiocarbon is $5730$ years.
\item If a relic contains $90\%$ as much radiocarbon as new material, can it have come from the time of Christ (approximately $2000$ years ago)? Note that the half-life of radiocarbon is $5730$ years.
\item The population of Cairo grew from $5$ million to $10$ million in $20$ years. Use an exponential model to find when the population was $8$ million.
\item The populations of New York and Los Angeles are growing at $1\%$ and $1.4\%$ a year, respectively. Starting from $8$ million (New York) and $6$ million (Los Angeles), when are the populations equal? Round your answer to a whole number of years.
\item Suppose the value of $\$1$ in Japanese yen decreases at $2\%$ per year. Starting from $\$1=\text{\textyen}250$, when will $\$1=\text{\textyen}1$?
\item The effect of advertising decays exponentially. If $40\%$ of the population remembers a new product after $3$ days, how long will $20\%$ remember it?
\item If $y=1000$ at $t=3$ and $y=3000$ at $t=4$, what was $y_{0}$ at $t=0$?
\item If $y=100$ at $t=4$ and $y=10$ at $t=8$, when does $y=1$?
\item If a bank offers annual interest of $7.5\%$ or continuous interest of $7.25\%$, which has a better annual yield?
\item What continuous interest rate has the same yield as an annual rate of $9\%$?
\item If you deposit $\$5000$ at $8\%$ annual interest, how many years can you withdraw $\$500$ (starting after the first year) without running out of money?
\item You are trying to save $\$50{,}000$ in $20$ years for college tuition for your child. If interest is a continuous $10\%$, how much do you need to invest initially?
\item You are cooling a turkey that was taken out of the oven with an internal temperature of $165^\circ \text{F}$. After $10$ minutes of resting the turkey in a $70^\circ \text{F}$ apartment, the temperature has reached $155^\circ \text{F}$. What is the temperature of the turkey $20$ minutes after taking it out of the oven?
\item You are trying to thaw some vegetables that are at a temperature of $1^\circ \text{F}$. To thaw vegetables safely, you must put them in the refrigerator, which has an ambient temperature of $44^\circ \text{F}$. You check on your vegetables $2$ hours after putting them in the refrigerator to find that they are now $12^\circ \text{F}$. Plot the resulting temperature curve and use it to determine when the vegetables reach $33^\circ \text{F}$.
\item You are an archaeologist and are given a bone that is claimed to be from a Tyrannosaurus Rex. You know these dinosaurs lived during the Cretaceous Era $(146$ million years to $65$ million years ago), and you find by radiocarbon dating that there is $0.000001\%$ the amount of radiocarbon. Is this bone from the Cretaceous?
\item The spent fuel of a nuclear reactor contains plutonium-239, which has a half-life of $24{,}000$ years. If $1$ barrel containing $10\,\text{kg}$ of plutonium-239 is sealed, how many years must pass until only $10\,\text{g}$ of plutonium-239 is left?
\end{enumerate}
