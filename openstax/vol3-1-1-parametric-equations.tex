For the following exercises, sketch the curves below by eliminating the parameter $t$. Give the orientation of the curve.

\begin{enumerate}[start=1]
\item $x=t^{2}+2t$, $y=t+1$
\item $x=\cos(t)$, $y=\sin(t)$, $t\in(0,2\pi]$
\item $x=2t+4$, $y=t-1$
\item $x=3-t$, $y=2t-3$, $1.5\le t\le 3$
\end{enumerate}

For the following exercises, eliminate the parameter and sketch the graphs.

\begin{enumerate}[resume]
\item $x=2t^{2}$, $y=t^{4}+1$
\end{enumerate}

For the following exercises, the pairs of parametric equations represent lines, parabolas, circles, ellipses, or hyperbolas. Name the type of basic curve that each pair of equations represents.

\begin{enumerate}[resume]
\item $\begin{array}{l}x=3t+4 \\ y=5t-2\end{array}$
\item $\begin{array}{l}x-4=5t \\ y+2=t\end{array}$
\item $\begin{array}{l}x=2t+1 \\ y=t^{2}-3\end{array}$
\item $\begin{array}{l}x=3\cos t \\ y=3\sin t\end{array}$
\item $\begin{array}{l}x=2\cos(3t) \\ y=2\sin(3t)\end{array}$
\item $\begin{array}{l}x=\cosh t \\ y=\sinh t\end{array}$
\item $\begin{array}{l}x=3\cos t \\ y=4\sin t\end{array}$
\item $\begin{array}{l}x=2\cos(3t) \\ y=5\sin(3t)\end{array}$
\item $\begin{array}{l}x=3\cosh(4t) \\ y=4\sinh(4t)\end{array}$

\item Use the equations in the preceding problem to find a set of parametric equations for a circle whose radius is 5 and whose center is $(-2,\,3)$.
\end{enumerate}

For the following exercises, use a graphing utility to graph the curve represented by the parametric equations and identify the curve from its equation.

\begin{enumerate}[resume]
\item \techrequired $\begin{array}{l}x=\theta+\sin \theta \\ y=1-\cos \theta \end{array}$
\item \techrequired $\begin{array}{l}x=2t-2\sin t \\ y=2-2\cos t\end{array}$
\item \techrequired $\begin{array}{l}x=t-0.5\sin t \\ y=1-1.5\cos t\end{array}$
\item An airplane traveling horizontally at $100\,\text{m}/\text{s}$ over flat ground at an elevation of $4000$ meters must drop an emergency package on a target on the ground. The trajectory of the package is given by $x=100t$, $y=-4.9t^{2}+4000$, $t\ge 0$, where the origin is the point on the ground directly beneath the plane at the moment of release. How many horizontal meters before the target should the package be released in order to hit the target?
\item The trajectory of a bullet is given by $x=v_{0}(\cos \alpha)t$, $y=v_{0}(\sin \alpha)t-\frac{1}{2}gt^{2}$, where $v_{0}=500\,\text{m}/\text{s}$, $g=9.8\,\text{m}/\text{s}^{2}$, and $\alpha=30^\circ$. When will the bullet hit the ground? How far from the gun will the bullet hit the ground?
\item \techrequired Use technology to sketch the curve represented by $x=\sin(4t)$, $y=\sin(3t)$, $0\le t\le 2\pi$.
\item \techrequired Use technology to sketch $x=2\tan(t)$, $y=3\sec(t)$, $-\pi<t<\pi$.
\item Sketch the curve known as an epitrochoid, which gives the path of a point on a circle of radius $b$ as it rolls on the outside of a circle of radius $a$.\\ The equations are $\begin{array}{l}x=(a+b)\cos t-c\cos\left(\frac{(a+b)t}{b}\right) \\ y=(a+b)\sin t-c\sin\left(\frac{(a+b)t}{b}\right).\end{array}$\\ Let $a=1$, $b=2$, and $c=1$.
\item \techrequired Use technology to sketch the spiral curve given by $x=t\cos(t)$, $y=t\sin(t)$, from $-2\pi\le t\le 2\pi$.
\item \techrequired Use technology to graph the curve given by the parametric equations $x=2\cot(t)$, $y=1-\cos(2t)$, $-\pi/2\le t\le \pi/2$. This curve is known as the witch of Agnesi.
\item \techrequired Sketch the curve given by the parametric equations $\begin{array}{l}x=\cosh(t) \\ y=\sinh(t)\end{array}$, where $-2\le t\le 2$.
\end{enumerate}
